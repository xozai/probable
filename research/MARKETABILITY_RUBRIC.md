# Marketability Rubric — the "ease of marketing" axis

Purpose: joseleos asked for candidate problems ranked on four axes. One of them —
"ease of marketing the solution" — is mine. This is the scoring instrument so that
axis is evidence-based rather than vibes. Owner of the overall ranking: HermesX.
Evidence supplier: Researcher.

Score each candidate 1–5 per dimension, multiply by weight, normalize to 100.

| # | Dimension | Weight | 5 = easy | 1 = hard |
|---|-----------|--------|----------|----------|
| 1 | **Named pain** — buyer already describes it publicly, unprompted | 3 | Engineers post workaround hacks monthly; real search demand | We must teach the category first |
| 2 | **Time-to-obvious** — value visible in one screenshot/GIF | 3 | Before/after is self-evident (plan sheet, takeoff, redline) | Value only appears after weeks, or requires trusting an invisible calc |
| 3 | **Buyer = user** — pain-feeler can actually pay | 3 | Individual PE / small-firm principal, card, under discretionary limit | Public agency; RFP or sole-source justification; 6–18mo procurement |
| 4 | **Channel fit** — cheap proven channels already reach them | 2 | 2+ channels reach the buyer directly | Buyer diffuse, or only reachable via incumbent-controlled channels |
| 5 | **Liability drag** (inverse) — how much the PE stamp slows the sale | 2 | Output not stamped; no design liability | Produces design values the engineer must trust — needs validation studies, insurer conversations, years of reputation |
| 6 | **Wedge narrative** — one sentence, no unwinnable competitor named | 2 | "It replaces the spreadsheet you built yourself" | "It's a better Civil 3D" — head-on with Autodesk/Bentley |
| 7 | **Reference velocity** — time to a nameable logo/testimonial | 1 | Private firms, will go on record | Public agencies that cannot endorse vendors |

## Why civil is unusually good on dimension 4

Channels that work in this vertical and are cheap:
- **PDH-credit webinars.** PEs need continuing-education hours to keep licensure. They
  will sit through a vendor webinar to get them. This is the single highest-leverage
  top-of-funnel motion in the space and almost nothing else in B2B software has an
  equivalent.
- State PE society newsletters and ASCE branch meetings.
- Eng-Tips forums, r/civilengineering.
- YouTube tutorial SEO — engineers search for "how do I do X in <tool>" constantly.
- Discipline conferences: TRB (transportation), WEFTEC (water/wastewater),
  Bentley YII, Autodesk University, ASCE Convention.

Discipline determines the channel set, so we must know which discipline each
candidate serves before we can score dimension 4.

## Anti-pattern to guard against in the ranking

Do not score an idea highly on marketability because the *market* is big. Big +
incumbent-owned + procurement-gated is the hardest marketing job in software.
Market size belongs on the "value to market" axis, not this one. Keep them separate
or the ranking double-counts and we talk ourselves into a fight with Bentley.

## What I need from Researcher, per candidate problem

1. **Verbatim pain quotes, 3–5, with source URLs.** Engineers describing the problem
   in their own words. These become headlines and ad copy. Paraphrases are useless
   to me — I need exact phrasing.
2. **Who holds budget:** individual / firm / agency, plus typical dollar authority.
3. **Current workaround:** self-built spreadsheet, manual process, incumbent tool,
   offshore drafting. This is what we position against.
4. **Does output touch a stamped deliverable?** Yes/no/assists. Drives dimension 5.
5. **Named incumbents + public pricing.**
6. **Where the audience gathers:** 2–3 specific channels, rough audience size.
7. **Discipline:** transportation / water-wastewater / structural / geotech /
   land development / construction.

Items 1 and 3 are the ones that are expensive to reconstruct later — capture them
during the discovery pass, not after.
